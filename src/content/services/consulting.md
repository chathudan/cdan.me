Sometimes you don't need another engineer — you need someone who's seen a lot of these decisions play out and can help you avoid the expensive mistakes. Fifteen years across backend, mobile, cloud, and Linux ops gives me a decent library of "seen that fail before" to draw on.

## What I take on

- **Architecture &amp; tech-choice reviews** before you commit to a direction. Cloud provider selection, monolith vs microservices, native vs cross-platform mobile, build vs buy, all the classics.
- **Technical due diligence** for investors, acquirers, or founders about to inherit a codebase they didn't write. Written recommendations, not just a call.
- **Code &amp; delivery audits** on existing systems that feel "off." Where's the tech debt actually costing you, and what's the pragmatic path forward.
- **Interim CTO / tech lead** engagements — usually a fixed number of hours per week for founders who need experienced technical judgment on tap while their team ramps up.
- **Hiring help** — writing job specs, reviewing take-home tests, interviewing candidates, sanity-checking offers.

## Good fits

- Non-technical founder about to hire their first engineer or agency and wants a second opinion.
- Team debating a rewrite or major platform migration.
- Investor doing technical DD on a portfolio company.
- Founder whose CTO just left and needs a bridge before the next hire.

I bill by the hour or the week for consulting, and I'll tell you plainly when a problem is outside my depth — the whole point is you get honest signal, not a sales pitch.
