Most stories about outsourcing end badly for one of two reasons: the wrong people were hired, or nobody set up the working system that lets a remote team stay in sync with the business. This work is about fixing both — from the first vetting call through a functioning squad you actually trust.

## What I bring

- **Sourcing &amp; vetting engineers directly**, not through agencies. I know what a good CV looks like in the regions I've worked in, and I can run the technical interview myself.
- **Team structure that fits your timezone** — pairing hours, standup cadence, async communication norms, and the tooling that makes it all work.
- **Onboarding &amp; standards** — engineering guidelines, code review culture, CI/CD, and the boring documentation that stops new hires asking the same questions on day 30.
- **Interim technical leadership** — I can be your team's tech lead while you find or promote one from within.
- **Honest reporting** — you get a clear read on team health, delivery velocity, and where the risks actually are.

## Good fits

- Founder in AU / US / EU wants to build an engineering team abroad and doesn't want to guess at every step.
- You've tried outsourcing once, it went sideways, and you want someone to structure it properly this time.
- You have a small in-house team and need to add capacity fast without diluting quality.

I don't run this as a body-shop — I build teams I'd be comfortable working alongside. That means fewer, better engagements, and honest advice about whether hiring abroad is even the right move for your situation.
