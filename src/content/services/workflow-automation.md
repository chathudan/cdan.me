Most teams have five to ten manual processes that shouldn't exist — someone exporting a CSV every Monday, someone copying data between two SaaS tools, someone re-typing the same email each week. This work is about finding those and making them go away, without introducing a new problem in the process.

## What I bring

- **Systems thinking** — I've been architecting production systems for 15 years. Automation isn't just wiring APIs together; it's designing something that stays reliable when the third party changes their schema.
- **Real integrations** — Stripe, PayPal, Salesforce, Recurly, Firebase, Google APIs, custom APIs of any shape. I've written most of these before.
- **The right tool for the job** — sometimes that's Zapier or Make. Sometimes it's n8n on your own infrastructure. Sometimes it's a small .NET service on Azure Functions because the SaaS options run out of runway. I pick based on cost, control, and who has to maintain it.
- **AI-in-the-loop, done properly** — LLMs for document extraction, smart routing, drafting, summarisation. Deployed with guardrails and monitoring, not vibes.
- **Internal tools &amp; dashboards** — when no-code hits its ceiling, a small purpose-built tool often beats another SaaS subscription.

## Good fits

- Your team is drowning in copy-paste work and you've tried no-code without success.
- You have an AI use-case in mind and want it built by someone who understands both the model and the production plumbing.
- You need an integration between two systems that don't play nicely and no off-the-shelf connector exists.

Two-day sprints are welcome — sometimes a focused engagement kills weeks of ongoing work.
